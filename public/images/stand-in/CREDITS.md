# Stand-in product photo credits

**Decision (2 Oct 2026, owner/business):** product tiles show generic photos of each
snack — not the owner's own product — so customers recognise them, until real
product photography exists.

**Photo direction (2 Oct 2026, client):** prefer photos taken in Nigeria / West Africa
(market stalls, hawkers' trays, home kitchens) over generic studio shots, wherever an
accurate photo of the snack exists. Rules:

- **Licensed sources only** (Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons / CC0, or
  Pexels). Never copied from other websites: their photos are copyrighted.
- No visible brand or label in the shot.
- Credited **publicly** on the site at **/credits** (required by CC BY-SA) and here.
- The shop states these are stand-ins, not Igbadun Bites' own products.

Real photos added to `public/images/products/` replace these automatically (see
docs/content-guide.md). Data and owner flags live in `lib/stand-in-images.ts`.
