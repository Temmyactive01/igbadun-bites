# Igbadun Bites — brand copy (draft for review)

Status: **draft v1, 2 Oct 2026 — for Temitope / the owner to review.**
Used in the Phase 1 homepage preview so it can be judged in place; nothing here is
final until approved.

**Honesty rules followed:** no invented founder or business history, no sourcing or
"handmade"/"family recipe" claims, no awards or reviews, no delivery-area promises.

owner has confirmed (pickup & delivery; custom packs for events, parties, gifting).
Lines marked **[OWNER]** need the owner's real story or facts.

## Chapter intros

### 01 — Chips
**Golden, thin and loud.**
Plantain and cocoyam chips with that unmistakable snap — the bag that never makes it
home unopened.

### 02 — Crunchy snacks
**The sound of every party tray.**
Chin chin, kokoro, akara, gurundi and groundnuts — for long journeys, late
conversations and "just one more handful".

### 03 — Traditional treats & sweets
**The sweets you saved for later.**
Coconut candy, baba dudu, condensed milk sweets and more — the ones you counted out in
your palm and made last all afternoon.
